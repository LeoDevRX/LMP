- Projecting pictures, like Substance Painter's planar projection: drag a picture onto the model and it's projected right where you dropped it, facing you, on a new fill layer. Handles on the model move it (middle), turn it (round knob) and resize it (square). Esc when you're done; "Move / turn / resize on the model" in Properties brings the handles back.
- Transparent PNGs keep their transparency: only the picture shows, on every channel of the layer (switchable in Properties).
- Fill layers have a new Planar projection mode with size, width / height, turn, "hide on back faces" and "repeat outside the box".
- New guide page: Projecting an image onto the model.
