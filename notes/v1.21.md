## LocalPaint v1.21

- Autosaves stay out of Windows' recent list. Right-clicking LocalPaint on the taskbar now only shows projects you saved or opened yourself, each once, newest 10. LocalPaint clears out the autosave entries older versions left there the first time it starts.
- The Recent tab on the start screen skips autosaves too. They're still under Autosaves.
- Restoring an autosave no longer adds it to Recent. Save it to put it back under its real name.
