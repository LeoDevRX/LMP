## LocalPaint v1.22

The layer right-click menu now works like Substance Painter's, and layers can have effects stacked on them.

**Right-click menu**
- Masks: add a white, black, bitmap (from a picture) or colour-selection mask, or a smart mask.
- Toggle, invert, invert background, clear or remove the mask.
- Create smart mask, Create smart material and Flatten layer (Ctrl+M). Flatten also works on folders.
- Export the mask to a file or the clipboard, or export the layer's channels as PNGs.
- Cut, copy, paste, duplicate, remove and group layers (Ctrl+X / C / V / D, Del, Ctrl+G).
- Colour tags.
- Copy mask and Paste into mask (Ctrl+Alt+C / Ctrl+Alt+V).
- Blending options.
- Add generator, paint, fill, levels, filter, colour selection or anchor point.

**Effects on layers**
- Filters, generators, levels and fills show as rows under the layer. Each has an eye, a blend mode, an opacity and an ×.
- Click an effect row to edit it. The channel buttons choose which channels it changes.
- Filters: blur, sharpen, invert, HSL, contrast / luminosity, clamp, grayscale, posterize, colour tint and gradient map.
- Generators: all of the Painter-style generators (Dirt, Metal Edge Wear, Light, Position…) plus LocalPaint's own.
- There's a new fx button at the top of the Layers panel for adding effects.

**Fixes**
- Saving a brush, material or smart material now asks for the name in LocalPaint's own window. The old box never opened in the desktop app.
