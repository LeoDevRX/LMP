- Substance materials (.sbsar) come in with all their settings, like Painter's Substance material mode: presets, seed, the same groups, colours, sliders, on/off switches and image inputs. Mesh map inputs (AO, curvature, world normals, position) are filled from your bakes.
- If Substance 3D Designer is installed, LMP uses Adobe's own renderer for them, so they look exactly like in Painter. You can point LMP at it in File > Settings.
- Simple Diffuse works fully without Adobe's renderer too, brush strokes and all. Other materials get a much better stand-in: panels, bricks, tiles, planks or lava cracks depending on the material.
- The materials from your Substance Painter show up on the shelf under Substance materials.
- Hold ~ over the 3D view for Blender-style camera views: Top, Bottom, Left, Right, Front, Back, 3/4 and Frame model.
- Deleting folders and paint layers no longer asks first (turn it back on in File > Settings > Layers). Ctrl+Z still brings them back.
- New export window, laid out like Painter's: Settings, Output templates and List of exports with a console.
- 34 output templates: Unity (Built-in, URP, HDRP), Unreal, Godot, Roblox, CryEngine / Lumberyard, Source 2, Lens Studio, Blender, Arnold, V-Ray, Redshift, Corona, RenderMan, Cinema 4D, KeyShot, Marmoset, glTF, Sketchfab, USD and more. Make your own by dragging maps onto channels.
- png (8 or 16 bits), jpg and tga; padding (dilation, transparent); an output folder you can see and open (D:\LMP\export by default when there's a D: drive).
