- Masks work like Substance Painter's now: Dirt, Metal Edge Wear, Dust, Mask Editor, Mask Builder, Light, Position, Ambient Occlusion and Curvature generators, with Painter's settings.
- Smart masks rebuilt from Painter's recipes (Dirt Cavities, Dust Occlusion, Edges Uber, Rust, Surface Worn and more), using free CC0 grunge scans that download the first time you use them.
- Painter smart masks (.spmsk) open in LocalPaint, and masks inside .spsm smart materials keep their whole stack.
- New project window: template, resolution, normal map format, baked maps you already have, and an option to bake right after importing.
- File > Export mesh: GLB with your textures inside, or OBJ.
- Projects are saved as .lp files now. Your .lmp and .tpaint files still open.
