## LocalPaint v1.20

Projected pictures can now be placed right in the 3D view, the way Substance Painter does it.

- A 3D manipulator sits on the projection: arrows to move it, rings to rotate it, cubes to stretch it on one axis (width or height).
- W / E / R switch between Move, Rotate and Scale while you're editing a projection.
- Drag on the model (not on a handle) to stick the projection to the surface under the cursor.
- Hold Shift to snap: small move steps, 15° turns, 0.1 scale steps.
- The top bar has Local / World axes, Reset and Done.
- To edit a projection later, select its layer and click "Edit in the viewport" under Projection in Properties. The sliders there still work and stay in sync.
