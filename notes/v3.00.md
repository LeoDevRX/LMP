- Substance materials (.sbsar) now come in with all their settings, like Painter's Substance material mode: presets, seed, the same groups, colours, sliders, on/off switches and image inputs. Mesh map inputs (AO, curvature, world normals, position) are filled from your bakes.
- If Substance 3D Designer is installed, LMP uses Adobe's own renderer (sbsrender) for these, so they look exactly like in Painter. You can point LMP at it in File > Settings.
- Simple Diffuse works fully without Adobe's renderer too, brush strokes and all.
- Other materials get a much better stand-in when Adobe's renderer isn't there: panels, bricks, tiles, planks or lava cracks depending on the material, and their sliders change it.
- The materials from your Substance Painter show up on the shelf under Substance materials.
- Tiling Substance materials go on tri-planar, so no UV seams.
