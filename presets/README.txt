TexPaint presets folder
=======================

Link this folder in TexPaint (Assets panel > "Link folder..."), e.g. D:\TexPaint\presets.
Anything you put here shows up on the Assets shelf after Refresh. Subfolders work (3 levels deep).

What goes here
- Smart materials (.tpsmart): fill layers + smart masks. Save your own with the smart icon under the shelf.
- Material presets (.tpmat): click to load into the brush or the selected fill layer.
- Texture sets (PNG / JPG): grouped by name + suffix (Brick_BaseColor.png, Brick_Roughness.png, _Metallic,
  _Height, _Normal, _Emissive), or a folder with color.jpg / rough.jpg / height.jpg inside.
  "Materials (Poly Haven)" has 19 free CC0 sets.
- HDR environments (.hdr): click one under Assets > Environments to light the scene.
  "HDRI (Poly Haven)" has 10 free CC0 HDRIs.
- Material Designer graphs (.tpgraph) and custom nodes (.tpnode): made in the Material Designer tab.
- Brushes (.tpbrush), and pictures inside a "brushes" folder become brush tips.
- Substance files: put your own .sbsar / .spsm files here and they show up under Substance materials.
  Only use files you have the rights to. .spp / .sppr can't be read.
